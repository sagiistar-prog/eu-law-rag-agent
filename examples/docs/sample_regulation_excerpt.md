# Fictional EU Product Safety Excerpt

This short sample is fictional and exists only for the safe demo.

A distributor should keep a product identification record, a supplier contact record, and a reference to the conformity document supplied with the product.

When a product category may affect consumer safety, the file should be checked by a qualified reviewer before a market decision is made.
